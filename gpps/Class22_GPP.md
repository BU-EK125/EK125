# Class 22 GPP: Explore Data with MATLAB Plots

Today's Group Practice Problem is an interactive MathWorks Academy tutorial
rather than a downloadable script. Work through it with your group in
MATLAB Online.

**Tutorial:** [Explore Data with MATLAB Plots](https://matlabacademy.mathworks.com/details/explore-data-with-matlab-plots/otmledp)

You'll need to create a free account with MathWorks using your BU
credentials to access the tutorial. It walks through the syntax of
`plot()` and `scatter()` through a series of short, interactive tasks.

**For the morning session,** complete the **Introduction**, **Visualize
Vectors**, and **Customize Axes** sections.

If you get stuck on any task, the tutorial has its own **"See Solution"**
link below each question -- use it before asking for help, and don't be
surprised if your group doesn't need many questions answered along the
way.
