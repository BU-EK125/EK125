# Class 24 GPP: Calculations with Vectors and Matrices

Today's Group Practice Problem is an interactive MathWorks Academy tutorial
rather than a downloadable script. Work through it with your group in
MATLAB Online.

**Tutorial:** [Calculations with Vectors and Matrices](https://matlabacademy.mathworks.com/details/calculations-with-vectors-and-matrices/otmlcvm)

You'll need a free MathWorks account (use your BU credentials) to access
the tutorial. It covers vector and matrix operations -- today's reading
topic.

Complete the sections: **Introduction**, **Operations on Arrays**,
**Statistical Operations on Matrices**, **Matrix Multiplication**, and
**Moving Window Calculations**.

The tutorial has its own **"See Solution"** link below each question if
your group gets stuck.
