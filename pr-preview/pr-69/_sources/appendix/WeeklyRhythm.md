# Weekly Rhythm of EK125

Please note that this is subject to change.

Normal weeks in the semester will consist of the following:

- Before each morning class, go online to the course site on BlackBoard (BB) to do all of the pre-class activities (this is likely to be a reading in the course e-book and a preassessment quiz). Answer all of the assessment questions **by 9AM on the morning of class.**

- **Morning**: class starts promptly with the IPP

  - Individual practice problems (IPP) are to be done within the first 5 minutes of class by yourself, without any references. These will be collected for attendance, graded using a simple numbering system (1=minimal understanding, 4=maximal understanding), and returned to you in the afternoon.

  - The remainder of "lecture" will be spent in small groups working on the Group Practice Problems (GPP) in groups of 3. These will be in the form of Google Colab notebooks. These will not be submitted for grading.

    - You are welcome to use references, but you are asked not to use AI to write the solution. You need to learn how to write the code yourself. Otherwise, you risk not being able to do so on written quizzes and exams.

- **Afternoon**: attend the afternoon "lab" session.

  - There may be a short lecture by the instructor on a supplementary coding "applications" topic for the day providing additional context to the syntax you're learning in the morning sessions.

  - A homework assignment, either problem-based or project-based will be assigned for the week.

  - Start work on this assignment in class and get help from TAs and the instructor.

- **Monday afternoon:** that week's homework is released

- **Before Friday's discussion**, prepare your reference index card for the quiz if there is one.

- **Friday**: attend your discussion section on time.

  - Quizzes will be given on paper during discussions; see dates on the Syllabus.

- **Sunday:** homework is due at 11:59PM unless otherwise noted in the assignment. This due date **may change** for project-style assignments.

- During exam weeks, prepare your reference sheet. Determine your exam room. Attend and ace the exam!
