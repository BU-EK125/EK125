# Class 23 GPP: Make and Manipulate Matrices

Today's Group Practice Problem is an interactive MathWorks Academy tutorial
rather than a downloadable script. Work through it with your group in
MATLAB Online.

**Tutorial:** [Make and Manipulate Matrices](https://matlabacademy.mathworks.com/details/make-and-manipulate-matrices/otmlmmm)

You'll need a free MathWorks account (use your BU credentials) to access
the tutorial. It covers creating and accessing vectors and matrices --
exactly today's reading topic.

Complete the sections: **Create Array Functions**, **Build New Matrices
from Existing Ones**, and **Extract and Modify Submatrices**.

The tutorial has its own **"See Solution"** link below each question if
your group gets stuck.
